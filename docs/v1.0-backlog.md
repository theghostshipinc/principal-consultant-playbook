## Release Objective

Publish a professional, reproducible, and recoverable F5 BIG-IP engineering lab that demonstrates architecture, implementation, high availability, application delivery, documentation, Git workflow, and operational discipline.

The v1.0 release focuses on building and documenting the lab platform.

Customer troubleshooting scenarios and Principal Consultant case studies will begin after the v1.0 platform is complete and published.
